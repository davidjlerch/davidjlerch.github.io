About Me

I am a researcher in the Human-AI Interaction Department at Fraunhofer IOSB and a PhD candidate at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT), specializing in computer vision, self-supervised learning, and human behavior recognition. My work focuses on developing AI systems that understand human cognitive states and behavior, particularly in automotive safety applications. I bridge the gap between laboratory research and real-world production, creating intelligent monitoring solutions that detect both visible and invisible aspects of human attention and distraction. By combining advanced sensing technologies with multimodal AI, I help create systems that don't just observe, they understand and adapt to human needs in real-time, contributing to safer and more intuitive human-machine interaction.
